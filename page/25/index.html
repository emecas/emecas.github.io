Living Open Source Foundation
https://www.livingopensource.net/

WHAT WE DO
Our mission is to foster the growth and use of the Open Source Software in Zambia and Africa!

Our approach is to create communities in countries where we have found motivated people that want to start an open source community. Working with the local open source enthusiasts, we organize events; find sponsors for venue, course materials, certification and logistics; organize an initial open source training and community building; help to certify community members with world-wide recognized high-standard Linux Foundation certification; and enable local talent to further educate and build the local communities.
