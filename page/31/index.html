https://github.com/OpenRefine/OpenRefine

OpenRefine (previously Google Refine) is a powerful tool for working with messy data: cleaning it; transforming it from one format into another; and extending it with web services and external data.

OpenRefine always keeps your data private on your own computer until YOU want to share or collaborate. Your private data never leaves your computer unless you want it to. (It works by running a small server on your computer and you use your web browser to interact with it)

The IEEE Mentoring Program is an online program that facilitates the matching of IEEE members for the purpose of establishing a mentoring partnership. Participation in the program is completely voluntary and open to all IEEE members (all grades) and Society Affiliates. To participate in the IEEE Mentoring Program, visit IEEE Collabratec: https://ieee-collabratec.ieee.org/?utm_source=MGA&utm_medium=Website&utm_campaign=MentorProgram_41215&utm_content=Link&utm_term=Organic&WT.mc_id=MGA_Website_Link_Organic_MentorProgram_41215® and sign in with your IEEE Account.
