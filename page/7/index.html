An open-source scientific and technical publishing system

Author using Jupyter notebooks or with plain text markdown in your favorite editor.
Create dynamic content with Python, R, Julia, and Observable.
Publish reproducible, production quality articles, presentations, dashboards, websites, blogs, and books in HTML, PDF, MS Word, ePub, and more.

Wolf Organization
https://share.google/aimode/4fmLADMoVfLIFMBpx

There are many documentaries that focus on wolf organizations and conservation efforts, including those covering reintroduction programs and advocacy groups. Several notable films include:

On specific organizations
Welcome Home (2024): This documentary from Reel Earth Films celebrates the reintroduction of wolves to Colorado after a public vote. It covers the story of the unique conservation success, featuring interviews with biologists, ranchers, and Native Americans.

Operation Wolf Patrol (2021): Eco-activist Rod Coronado, a Native American, is the subject of this film. He documents his fight to protect gray wolves being hunted in the U.S. by following and confronting hunters through his organization, Wolf Patrol.
