Moving from Buttercup to KeePassXC: not by choice, but by necessity

emecas published on 2026-11-24 included in Security and Privacy Developer Tools Productivity and Apps

For a long time Buttercup was my password manager. It was simple, open source, and it fit the way I worked. So when its maintainer, Perry Mitchell, announced on March 3, 2025 that the project was ending, it wasn’t something I could ignore. He explained that since co-founder Sallar left, he had been the only active maintainer, and that the project’s scope, across operating systems, mobile platforms and third-party services, with ongoing security requirements, was too much for one person. On June 26, 2025 the desktop app’s repository was archived and is now read-only, like the rest of the project.
