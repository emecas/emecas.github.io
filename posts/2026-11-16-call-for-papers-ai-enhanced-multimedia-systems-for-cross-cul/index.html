Call for Papers: AI-Enhanced Multimedia Systems for Cross-Cultural Communication and Social Impact 2026-10-05
https://www.ijimai.org/index.php/ijimai/announcement/view/15

The International Journal of Interactive Multimedia and Artificial Intelligence (IJIMAI) invites researchers and practitioners to submit their contributions to the special section: AI-Enhanced Multimedia Systems for Cross-Cultural Communication and Social Impact, edited by Gwanggil Jeon, Richard Chbeir and Mahmoud Barhamgi.

The special section aims to showcase groundbreaking research on the integration of AI in multimedia systems to Foster cross-cultural communication and drive social impact. The focus is on leveraging AI to bridge cultural divides, enhance inclusivity, and amplify diverse narratives, while also addressing the ethical challenges and potential biases introduced by AI-generated content. This initiative seeks to advance the scientific understanding of AI’s transformative role in shaping culturally adaptive multimedia platforms and to provide insights into the future of AI-driven communication in an increasingly interconnected global landscape.

Important Dates:

Paper submission deadline: January 15, 2027.
First-round review notification: February 28, 2027.
Revision due: April 30, 2027.
Final approval by the Editor-in-Chief: June 30, 2027.
